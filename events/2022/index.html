FreeBSD Past Events

Please send details of any FreeBSD related events or events that are of interest for FreeBSD users which are not listed here to freebsd-doc@FreeBSD.org.

Past Events:

December 2022

  • An Introduction to FreeBSD Services – Drew Gurkowski, FreeBSD Fridays – The FreeBSD Foundation, ,
    16 - 16 December, 2022
    Setting up printing, audio, and Wi-Fi on a fresh FreeBSD install.

November 2022

  • November 2022 FreeBSD Vendor Summit, Online, ,
    03 - 04 November, 2022
    Join us for the online November 2022FreeBSD Vendor Summit. The event will consist of virtual, half day sessions, taking place November 3-4, 2022. It’s free to attend, but we ask that you register with the eventbrite system to gain access to the meeting room and separate hallway track. The vendor summit is sponsored by the FreeBSD Foundation.

October 2022

  • Aberdeen Hackathon 2022, 57 North Hacklab, Aberdeen, Scotland
    08 - 08 October, 2022
    A FreeBSD Hackathon will take place in Aberdeen, Scotland from the 5th of October to the 8th of October 2022. The hackathon will be hosted by 57 North Hacklab.

September 2022

  • EuroBSDcon 2022, The Freihaus Campus of Vienna's Technical University, Vienna, Austria
    15 - 18 September, 2022
    EuroBSDcon is the European annual technical conference gathering users and developers working on and with 4.4BSD (Berkeley Software Distribution) based operating systems family and related projects.

June 2022

  • June 2022 FreeBSD Developer Summit, Online, ,
    16 - 17 June, 2022
    Join us for the online June 2022 FreeBSD Developer Summit. The event will consist of virtual, half day sessions, taking place June 16-17, 2022. It is free to attend, but we ask that you register with the conference system to gain access to the meeting room (more information to come). In addition to developer discussion sessions, we will also have vendor talks.

  • BSDCan 2022, Online, ,
    01 - 04 June, 2022
    A four day BSD conference held online. BSDCan hosts talks and tutorials on a range of topics based around the BSD family of operating systems.

March 2022

  • Google Summer of Code, FreeBSD Office Hours – YouTube, ,
    16 - 16 March, 2022
    The then upcoming Google Summer of Code, and how mentees could become involved.

February 2022

  • How to Track FreeBSD Using Git, Part 2 – Warner Losh, FreeBSD Fridays – YouTube, ,
    11 - 11 February, 2022
    A primer on how to track FreeBSD using Git. The basics of obtaining and compiling FreeBSD, then more advanced topics – including managing local changes, and how to contribute those changes to the FreeBSD Project.

  • FOSDEM 2022, Online, ,
    05 - 06 February, 2022
    Every year, thousands of developers of free and open source software from all over the world gather at the event in Brussels. In 2022, they will gather online. Be sure to stop by the FreeBSD stand and BSD devroom to get the latest on FreeBSD.
